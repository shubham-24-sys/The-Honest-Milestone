Understanding the Dip Mechanics

In a standard equity SIP, a market crash is actually your friend if your goal is 5 to 10 years away.

Rupee-Cost Averaging:

When the Net Asset Value (NAV) of a fund drops from ₹100 to ₹78, your fixed ₹3,000 monthly investment buys 38.4 units instead of 30 units.

The Recovery Spring:

When markets inevitably rebound over subsequent years, those extra accumulated units supercharge your portfolio's growth.
